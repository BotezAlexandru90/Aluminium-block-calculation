💡 Quick Guide & Parameter Map

Welcome! This application calculates direct manufacturing costs using precise engineering math. Here is where to locate key variables:

🎯 Drilling & Standard Holes: To adjust hole metrics, look inside Section 3 (Machining & Cycle Time). At the bottom of that section, you can define the total quantity of holes and the average cycle duration (in seconds) per hole.

🛠️ Save & Backup: Use the buttons in the header to save configurations to your browser cache, or export/import them as .json templates.

⚖️ Material Weighing: In Section 1, toggle "Method" to switch between dimensional volume calculations and a manual weight override.

⚙️ Post-Machining Options: In Section 5, you can add dynamic cleaning, deburring, and surface treatments calculated by weight, run-time, or flat fees.
